Highway Hungama – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Highway Hungama ("the game") is an offline racing game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The app does not request the Internet permission and works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of game data only on your own device: your in-game money, the bikes you own, the route you have reached and your sound setting. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the game.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts collecting any information (for example, if online features are added), this policy will be updated before that change is released, and the date above will change.
